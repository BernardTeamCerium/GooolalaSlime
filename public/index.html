Gooo Care

🫙

Lid on tight

Close the jar after every squish so your slime stays soft and stretchy.

🧼

Clean hands

Wash hands before and after play. Clean hands keep slime looking royal.

🛋️

Play on smooth spots

Keep slime away from carpet, clothes and hair. Tables are best!

🚫

Do not eat

Slime is a toy, not a snack. Grown-up help recommended for little royals.
